2. Data we process

Depending on which features you use, the Service may process:

  • Discord account and server data: user ID, username, display name, avatar, server ID, channel ID, role information and server membership information;
  • interaction data: commands, message content, message IDs, timestamps, reactions and links to Discord messages;
  • suggestion data: the text and attachments you submit, your Discord mention and user ID, votes, and status or notes added by moderators;
  • ticket data: the ticket reason, messages and attachments posted in a private ticket, and records of who opened or closed the ticket; and
  • age-verification data, when enabled: a deliberately redacted image showing only the age or date of birth needed for a manual check, plus any note used to link the submission to your Discord account.

The bot does not maintain a separate user-profile database in its current implementation. Relevant content is processed within Discord channels and messages. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising or cross-service profiling.

3. Why we process data and our legal bases

We process personal data only for the following purposes:

  • Providing requested features, such as creating a ticket or publishing a suggestion. The legal basis is performance of our agreement with you or steps taken at your request.
  • Operating and securing the Service, enforcing rules, preventing abuse, troubleshooting and protecting users. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing a safe and reliable community service.
  • Checking eligibility for age-restricted areas, where enabled by a server. The legal basis is our legitimate interest, and the relevant server’s legitimate interest, in restricting access to age-appropriate community spaces and protecting users. A moderator performs the check; there is no solely automated decision-making.
  • Complying with law and responding to valid legal requests. The legal basis is compliance with a legal obligation or, where applicable, our legitimate interest in establishing, exercising or defending legal claims.

Providing command or suggestion content is optional, but the requested feature may not work without it. Providing the minimum evidence required for age verification is optional; if you do not provide it, you may be unable to access the relevant age-restricted server area.

4. Important rules for age-verification material

Do not upload a full or uncensored identity document. Before uploading, obscure all information that is not needed to confirm your age, including:

  • photograph;
  • full name and signature;
  • home address and nationality;
  • document number, expiry date and issuing authority;
  • personal identification or social-security number, including a Dutch BSN; and
  • machine-readable text, QR codes, barcodes or similar identifiers.

Only the age or date of birth needed for the check should remain visible. Add a watermark with the date, the relevant server name and “age verification only”. If you accidentally upload an uncensored document, close the ticket or contact us immediately at diegoreif2004@gmail.com and ask for deletion.

Verification material is visible only to the user, the bot, and members given access to the private ticket channel by the relevant Discord server. Server owners must restrict this access to authorised moderators.

5. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the relevant purpose:

  • Closed tickets and their attachments: the current bot configuration schedules the private ticket channel for deletion approximately 10 minutes after it is closed. An authorised moderator may delete it sooner. Open tickets remain available until closed or deleted.
  • Suggestions: remain in the server’s suggestion channel until a server administrator or moderator deletes them, the server removes the channel, or a valid deletion request is completed.
  • Discord account, server and interaction data: accessed as needed to respond to events and commands and not stored by the bot in a separate persistent database in the current implementation.
  • Security or legal records: if a specific incident requires information to be retained, we keep only what is necessary for the applicable investigation, legal obligation or claim, then delete or anonymise it.

Deleting a Discord message or channel removes it from the Service’s accessible workspace. Discord may retain information for a different period under its own policies, which we do not control.
